# Case Report: Grade 1 Semitendinosus Strain – Central Midfielder

## Incident Summary
Player reported tightness and mild pain in the posterior thigh during the warm-up before a league match. Removed from starting line-up as precaution.

## Initial Diagnosis
- **Injury**: Grade I semitendinosus strain.
- **Mechanism**: Sprint during pre-match activation drills.
- **Imaging**: Not required; clinical exam only (Grade I, minimal functional loss).

## Treatment Timeline
| Day | Intervention |
|-----|-------------|
| 0-2 | RICE, isometric holds, no stretching |
| 3-5 | Walk-jog progression, Nordic exercises (reduced range) |
| 6-8 | Pain-free sprinting at 70%, non-contact drills |
| 9-11 | High-speed GPS running benchmarks (club POL-GPS-01 thresholds) |
| 12 | Cleared for match squad selection |

## Outcome
Player returned to match squad within **12 days** after passing high-speed GPS running benchmarks. Played 45 minutes in first match back. No recurrence within 3 months.

## Clinical Insight
Accelerated rehab using PROT-HAM-01 Phase 2 criteria and strict GPS load caps enabled early return for Grade I strain. Club policy required passing high-speed running benchmarks before match clearance.
