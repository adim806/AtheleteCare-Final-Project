# Case Report: Grade 2 Biceps Femoris Strain – Winger

## Incident Summary
Player felt sharp pain in the back of the thigh during a high-speed sprint in training. Unable to complete the session. No audible pop. Pulled from afternoon tactical work.

## Initial Diagnosis
- **Injury**: Grade II biceps femoris strain (proximal musculotendinous junction).
- **Mechanism**: Sprint acceleration at end of high-intensity training block.
- **Imaging**: MRI confirmed partial tear; no avulsion.

## Treatment Timeline
| Day | Intervention |
|-----|-------------|
| 0-3 | RICE, isometric holds per PROT-HAM-01 Phase 1 |
| 4-7 | Pool running, hydrotherapy sessions (2× weekly) |
| 8-14 | Nordic eccentric progression, walk-jog intervals |
| 15-18 | Non-contact training, pain-free sprinting at 70% |
| 19-21 | Full-speed sprinting, sport-specific drills |
| 22 | Return to full team training |

## Outcome
Player returned to full training on **day 22** without re-injury. Acute:chronic workload ratio kept below 1.5 in first week back. No match minutes until day 28.

## Clinical Insight
Combination of PROT-HAM-01 with club hydrotherapy protocol reduced swelling and allowed earlier eccentric loading from day 8. Timeline aligned with club average for Grade II hamstring strains (18-28 days).
