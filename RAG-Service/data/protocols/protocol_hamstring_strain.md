# Hamstring Strain Management Protocol

## Classification
- **Grade I**: Mild strain, minimal loss of function, no palpable defect.
- **Grade II**: Partial tear, moderate pain, reduced strength.
- **Grade III**: Complete rupture, significant loss of function.

## Initial Assessment (0-72 hours)
1. Clinical examination: palpation, range of motion, strength testing.
2. Imaging: MRI recommended for Grade II/III or if return-to-play timeline exceeds 3 weeks.
3. Pain score and functional deficit documented in the player medical record.

## Treatment Phases

### Phase 1 – Acute (Days 0-7)
- RICE protocol (Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation).
- Avoid stretching and aggressive manual therapy.
- Isometric hamstring holds at pain-free angles.
- Target pain score < 3/10 before progression.

### Phase 2 – Sub-acute (Days 7-21)
- Progressive eccentric loading (Nordic hamstring exercises).
- Running progression: walk-jog intervals on flat surface.
- Core and glute activation exercises.
- Return to non-contact training when pain-free sprinting at 70% achieved.

### Phase 3 – Return to Play (Days 21+)
- Full-speed sprinting and change-of-direction drills.
- Sport-specific drills with medical clearance.
- GPS load monitoring: acute:chronic workload ratio kept below 1.5.

## Return-to-Play Criteria
- Full range of motion (within 5° of contralateral limb).
- Isokinetic strength ≥ 90% of uninjured side.
- Pain-free at maximum effort sprint.
- Medical sign-off from club physiotherapist and team doctor.

## Historical Note
Similar cases at the club have averaged 18-28 days return-to-play for Grade II strains with early eccentric loading.
