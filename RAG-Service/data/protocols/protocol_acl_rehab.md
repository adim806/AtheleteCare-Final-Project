# Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) Rehabilitation Protocol

## Overview
Post-surgical ACL reconstruction rehabilitation follows a phased, criteria-based progression model aligned with FIFA medical guidelines.

## Phase 1 – Protection (Weeks 0-6)
- Weight-bearing as tolerated with crutches (typically 2-4 weeks partial WB).
- Quad sets, straight leg raises, patellar mobilisation.
- Knee flexion target: 0-90° by week 4, 0-120° by week 6.
- Swelling management: cryotherapy, compression, elevation.

## Phase 2 – Strength & ROM (Weeks 6-12)
- Closed-chain exercises: mini squats, leg press (0-60°), step-ups.
- Proprioception: single-leg balance, wobble board.
- Stationary cycling from week 6.
- Target: full extension, flexion ≥ 125°.

## Phase 3 – Running & Agility (Weeks 12-24)
- Treadmill running from week 12 (if quad strength ≥ 70% LSI).
- Linear sprint progression: 50% → 75% → 100% over 4 weeks.
- Lateral movements and cutting drills from week 16.
- Hop tests: single-hop, triple-hop, crossover hop (LSI ≥ 85%).

## Phase 4 – Return to Sport (Months 6-9)
- Full team training with modified contact.
- Psychological readiness assessment (ACL-RSI score ≥ 56).
- Isokinetic quad/hamstring ratio ≥ 0.6 at 60°/s.
- Medical clearance required before competitive match play.

## Key Risk Factors for Re-injury
- Returning before 9 months post-surgery.
- Quad strength deficit > 20% limb symmetry index.
- Incomplete psychological readiness.

## Club Benchmark
Players following this protocol have returned to full training at a median of 8.5 months post-ACL reconstruction.
