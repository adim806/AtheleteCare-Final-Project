# Club GPS Load Management Policy

## High-Speed Running Thresholds
- **Match trigger**: A player exceeding **800 metres** of high-speed running (>19.8 km/h) in a competitive match must enter a monitored recovery protocol the following day.
- **Training cap**: High-speed running volume in the first training session post-match is capped at **60%** of the player's 28-day rolling average.
- **Sprint count**: More than 15 maximal sprints (>25 km/h) in a single session requires medical staff notification before the next high-intensity day.

## Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio
- Target acute:chronic (A:C) ratio range: **0.8 – 1.3** during normal training blocks.
- **Maximum A:C ratio: 1.5** — mandatory load reduction if exceeded (applies during hamstring RTP; see PROT-HAM-01).
- Weekly review of A:C trends for all players in the injury database.

## Post-Match Recovery Day Rules
- Mandatory recovery session on the day after a match for players with >800 m high-speed running.
- Training duration capped at **45 minutes** on recovery day; no maximal sprinting.
- Pool recovery or low-impact cycling preferred over pitch running.

## Return-from-Injury Load Caps
- First match back: high-speed running limited to **70%** of pre-injury 28-day average unless cleared by medical staff.
- First two matches post-RTP: monitor A:C ratio daily; flag if >1.3.
- Cross-reference with linked protocol (e.g. PROT-HAM-01) for injury-specific caps.
