# Club Post-Match and Recovery Policy

## Ice and Compression Protocol
- Immediate post-match: **15 minutes** ice application to major muscle groups (quadriceps, hamstrings, calves) in the dressing room.
- Compression garments worn for **2 hours** post-match on travel days.
- No aggressive manual therapy or deep tissue massage within **24 hours** of competitive match play.

## Sleep Requirements
- **Minimum 8 hours** sleep on match night; tracked via club wellness app.
- Match week (MD-1 to MD+1): target **9 hours** for players logging >750 m total distance.
- Sleep debt >2 hours below target triggers reduced training load next day (coordination with POL-GPS-01).

## Hydration Protocol
- Weigh-in before and after match; **>2% body mass loss** requires structured rehydration plan (150% of fluid deficit over 4-6 hours).
- Electrolyte replacement mandatory after matches played above 28°C ambient temperature.
- No alcohol within 24 hours of match completion (club code of conduct).

## Long-Haul Travel Recovery
- Flights exceeding **3 hours**: mandatory mobility session within 2 hours of landing.
- Match within 72 hours of long-haul travel: medical review required before full training participation.
- Compression travel socks mandatory for flights >2 hours during congested fixture periods.
