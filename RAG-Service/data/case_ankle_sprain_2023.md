# Case Report: Lateral Ankle Sprain – Player #14

## Incident Summary
Player sustained a lateral ankle sprain during a competitive match following an inversion mechanism while contesting a header. Immediate swelling and inability to weight-bear observed pitch-side.

## Initial Diagnosis
- **Injury**: ATFL (anterior talofibular ligament) sprain, Grade II.
- **Mechanism**: Inversion, plantarflexion.
- **Imaging**: X-ray negative for fracture; ultrasound confirmed partial ATFL tear.

## Treatment Timeline
| Day | Intervention |
|-----|-------------|
| 0   | RICE, ankle brace, crutches (partial WB) |
| 3   | Early mobilisation, proprioception exercises |
| 7   | Pool running, cycling |
| 14  | Jogging on treadmill, lateral band walks |
| 21  | Return to non-contact training |
| 28  | Full return to match play |

## Outcome
Player returned to full match availability at **28 days**. No recurrence in the following season. Star excursion balance test (SEBT) reached 95% of baseline by day 21.

## Clinical Insight
Early protected mobilisation (from day 3) and proprioceptive training accelerated return-to-play compared to the club average of 35 days for Grade II lateral ankle sprains.
